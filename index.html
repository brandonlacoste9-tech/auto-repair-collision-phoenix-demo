Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome during our hours, Monday through Saturday 9 AM to 5 PM. For bigger jobs like engine rebuilds or collision work, calling ahead gets you in faster.

Do you repair all makes and models?

Yes — cars, SUVs and light trucks of all makes.

How does a collision repair estimate work?

Bring the vehicle in (or call us) and we'll look it over, explain what it needs, and confirm the price before any work starts.

What are your hours?

Monday through Saturday, 9 AM to 5 PM. Closed Sundays.
